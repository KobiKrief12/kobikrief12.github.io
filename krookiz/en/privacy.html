Your recipes stay yours

Krookiz has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The developer cannot read your recipes.

Effective November 20, 2026 · applies to Krookiz for iPhone

What is stored on the device

Your catalogs, recipes, favorites, post cover pictures and the screenshots you add are kept in the app's storage on your iPhone.

Reading a recipe from a link

When you share a post from Instagram, TikTok, Facebook or a recipe website to Krookiz, the app opens that page directly from your phone to read its caption, ingredients and cover picture. The service hosting the post sees an ordinary request from your phone, as when you open the link in a browser. None of it passes through a Krookiz server.

Sync and sharing through iCloud

So that several people can see the same recipe book, and so that it comes back after reinstalling, each phone keeps a copy of it in Apple's iCloud. Before the copy leaves the device it is encrypted with a key derived from your sharing code.

  • The developer can see that encrypted records exist, but does not have the code and cannot read them.
  • Anyone you send an invitation link or the code to joins the recipe book: they can see and change the recipes and also receive the AI key, if you entered one. Send it only to people you want to share with.
  • The invitation link points to a page on this site (hosted on GitHub Pages). The code is in the part of the link after the # sign, which browsers do not send to the server.
  • The sharing code is also kept in the keychain and in iCloud key-value storage in your account, so your recipes come back after reinstalling.

Sync requires iCloud on the device, and Apple's privacy policy applies to it as well.

Smart sorting with AI (optional)

This feature is off until you enter your own Google Gemini API key in the settings. When it is on, the text of the post you share, and sometimes the screenshots you picked, are sent to Google through your own account and key to be turned into a recipe.

  • Google's terms and privacy policy apply to that data. On the free tier, for example, Google may use the content to improve its products.
  • The key is stored in the device keychain and travels to the other devices sharing the recipe book inside the encrypted iCloud copy. It is never sent to the developer.
  • Removing the key in the settings turns the feature off.

Photos

The photo library is used only when you pick screenshots of a recipe. The first screenshot is kept on the device as the recipe's picture.

Timers

The alert when a step's timer ends is scheduled on the device itself. The app sends no notifications from a server.

Sharing and export

When you share a recipe or export all recipes (a Word file, a text file or a copy), the file is made on the device and goes only where you send it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party libraries, no advertising and no analytics or crash reporting tools. If you chose in iOS settings to share analytics with developers, Apple may show the developer aggregated, anonymous usage and crash data.

Deleting data

Any recipe or catalog can be deleted in the app, and the deletion also reaches the iCloud copy and the other devices sharing the recipe book. Deleting the app removes what is stored on the device. For anything else, contact us.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
