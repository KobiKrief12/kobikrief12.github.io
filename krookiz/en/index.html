All the recipes you love, in one place

See a recipe on Instagram, TikTok or Facebook? Share it to Krookiz, pick a catalog, and it is laid out for you: ingredients, steps, and amounts that follow the number of servings. The app is in Hebrew.

Saving a recipe

  • In the post, tap the share button and choose Krookiz. The first time, Krookiz is at the end of the row of apps, under "More".
  • Pick a catalog and you are done. The recipe is laid out when you open the app.
  • You can also add from inside the app with the + button (on the main screen or inside a catalog): paste a link or text, or pick screenshots.

Smart sorting with AI

With no setup Krookiz does a basic layout. With a free Google Gemini key (Settings, then the button to get a free key from Google) it understands any post: it translates English recipes, separates ingredients from steps even when everything is written together, and reads screenshots. A key entered on one phone reaches everyone sharing the recipes.

Sharing the recipe book

In Settings, "שיתוף" (sharing), send an invitation link. Whoever receives it taps it, confirms, and from then on you both see the same catalogs and recipes.
